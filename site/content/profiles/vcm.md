Vancouver, Coast & Mountains appears on the official tourism map for geographic context. Destination BC, a provincial Crown corporation, directly administers the region. Its governance structure and communicative position differ materially from the five independently managed regional organizations selected for this study.

For this reason the region remains visibly outside the comparative language and board corpus. The map uses diagonal hatching to distinguish the excluded region without erasing its geography.

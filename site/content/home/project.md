# Project

Who speaks for a destination? examines how British Columbia's independently managed regional tourism organizations describe their roles, priorities and intended beneficiaries. The project compares dated About pages instead of treating public relations language as evidence of institutional outcomes.

The language viewer preserves the original wording and lets readers inspect clause-level annotations. The governance viewer will provide a separate account of board membership and professional affiliations as documented records become available. Together, these materials permit a cautious comparison of institutional self-description and representation.

Every historical comparison is limited to recorded source dates. Changes between captures are observations, not claims about every intervening year.

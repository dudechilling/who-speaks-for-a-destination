# B.C.'s RDMOs

Five independently managed regional destination organizations form the study: Northern BC Tourism Association, Cariboo Chilcotin Coast Tourism Association, Thompson Okanagan Tourism Association, Kootenay Rockies Tourism, and the Vancouver Island organization now associated with 4VI.

Their websites describe tourism development, marketing and stewardship from different institutional positions. Historical and contemporary About pages provide a comparable starting point for examining claimed beneficiaries and priorities. Board records will supply a separate governance comparison.

The provincial map includes Vancouver, Coast & Mountains for context. Its region is administered directly by Destination BC, a Crown corporation, and is excluded from the five-organization comparison.

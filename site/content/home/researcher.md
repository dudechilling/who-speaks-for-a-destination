# Researcher

The research process separates recorded source text, model-proposed linguistic tags and interpretations accepted by a human reviewer. The coding workbook is a working research instrument, not an independent authority on what an organization meant or whom it actually benefited.

The public tables display thematic findings only when their evidence and review status are established. Visitors may import a compatible workbook to explore additional source years. The private adjudicator remains a separate, authenticated route for the researcher's decisions.

Source links and methodological limits accompany the material wherever possible. Reconstructing a historical About page does not establish continuity in governance or policy between captures.

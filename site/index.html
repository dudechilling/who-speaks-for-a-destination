THE SOURCE SET

Organizations, across two moments

Select an organization to compare its historical and contemporary page records. Dates are archive capture or retrieval dates—not inferred publication dates.

LANGUAGE

Reading institutional language with care

This project uses Systemic Functional Linguistics (SFL) to describe process, agency, and modality: what kinds of action or relation are represented, who is positioned as acting or affected, and how obligation, possibility, or certainty is expressed.

Critical discourse analysis (CDA) supports attention to stated priorities, beneficiaries, and forms of legitimation. These are analytic lenses—not evidence that a page’s language proves organizational intent, performance, or power.

GOVERNANCE

Representation is a question, not a conclusion.

Board comparison research is forthcoming. A website’s named people, partners, or constituencies do not on their own establish formal representation, voting rights, or governance authority.

METHODS & SOURCES

Provenance before interpretation

Selective normalization

Navigation may be removed selectively. Text is not rewritten, lowercased, paraphrased, or duplicated into a second “normalized” field.

Page-level provenance

Each retained element remains linked to a captured page, source URL, and recorded capture or retrieval date. Unknown publication dates stay unknown.

Machine and human review

Initial machine suggestions are separate from human adjudication. Researcher actions, source verification, and code decisions are recorded privately.

Long source text and review records are deliberately unavailable here. Authorized researchers can use the private adjudicator on the tailnet.

## 4. Periods without located evidence
- **Cariboo Chilcotin Coast:** no dated candidate for 1990–1994 or 1995–1999.
- **Every region before 1998:** no association-authored annual report, strategic plan, financial statement or board record was located.

Region by region, the downloaded pre-1999 evidence of each region is mostly *other bodies' records about the associations*: Hansard sittings and indexes, newspaper OCR items, provincial reports and region profiles. Documents the associations produced themselves before 1999 amount to:
- Vancouver Island: 1998 islands.bc.ca pages (board list, AGM material, newsletters, a letter);
- Kootenay Rockies: one 1998 Tourism Rockies item;
- Thompson Okanagan, Northern BC and Cariboo Chilcotin Coast: none.

These counts come from a keyword heuristic over document type and source class, so they should be checked by hand.

**1999–2012.** Association-authored records exist but are uneven:
- Vancouver Island and Northern BC: strong (board, AGM, newsletters, governance).
- Kootenay Rockies: board rosters and newsletters.
- Cariboo Chilcotin Coast: from 2003.
- Thompson Okanagan: thin after 2006.

## 5. Gaps by proposed dataset (provisional mapping)
- **Dataset A — language.**
  - The preferred source type, annual reports, exists only from about 2015 (Kootenay Rockies) or 2020–2023 (the other four).
  - For 1998–2012, comparable public texts are mainly newsletters, AGM material, web "about" pages and marketing guides.
  - Strategic plans are few and recent: 1–5 per region, mostly 2019–2024.
- **Dataset B — governance.**
  - Best covered: rosters, elections and AGM records from about 1998–2003 onward in VI, NB and CC; KR rosters for 1999–2003 and 2009–2013; TO board pages to 2016.
  - Board minutes: NB 2001–2007, CC 2003 and the 2020s, KR 2021–2025 only.
  - Bylaws exist from about 2001.
  - Before 1998, governance evidence is limited to newspaper reports of AGMs and elections (KBVA and KCTA) and Hansard mentions of association presidents.
- **Dataset C — performance.**
  - Financial statements: CC 2005 and 2018–2026; KR 2022–2025; TO four items; VI one undated item; **NB none**.
  - Destination BC agreements (FY2013/14 on) and service plan reports give funder-defined measures from 2012.
  - The associations' own reported performance measures before 2010 are rare.
  - Many deliverables (regional operations plans, quarterly performance reports) were reported to the funder and not published. Their outcome is "requires human contact".

## 6. Which comparisons the evidence may support, and which may be unreliable
This section describes evidence availability only. It does not recommend a design; design decisions belong to the researcher.

**May be supported by located evidence** (subject to the researcher's review of each document):
- Comparisons within the 2000–2026 period for Vancouver Island, Northern BC and Cariboo Chilcotin Coast, using recurring genres: AGM material, board lists and elections, newsletters, bylaws, governance policies, marketing plans.
- Cross-regional comparison in the 2013–2026 period using parallel documents: Destination BC regional agreements (all five regions from FY2013/14), destination development strategies (about 2017–2020), service plan reports.
- Board-composition records (Dataset B) for about 1998–2013 in VI and NB, about 2003–2009 and 2020s in CC, and 1999–2003 and 2009–2013 in KR.

**May be unreliable or unsupported with the located evidence:**
- Any 30–40-year diachronic comparison of the associations' *own* language. Before about 1998 almost no association-authored text was located, so early "language" evidence would rest on provincial documents, Hansard and newspaper reports. Those are different genres with different authors.
- Annual-report-to-annual-report comparison over time: annual reports exist only from 2015 or later.
- Comparisons involving Thompson Okanagan for 2006–2015, or Cariboo Chilcotin Coast before 2003: the record is thin.
- Performance comparison across regions before 2013: financial and performance records are sparse and inconsistent by region; NB has none.
- Any comparison that treats records from different genres or issuers as equivalent (for example a 1980s provincial region profile against a 2020s association strategy).
- Any inference from absence. A missing year or document type often reflects archive survival, login-only member areas or unpublished deliverables.

**Uncertain metadata that affects comparisons:**
- 524 rows have no publication year.
- 400 rows have `date_status = uncertain`.
- 16 rows rest on page-image readings or unconfirmed claims.
- 15 Wayback files could not be matched to a capture by digest, and 34 have no capture listed for their original URL.
- `metadata_confidence` is an agent assessment, and no human has reviewed it yet.

- **Human contact (80 leads):** board minutes, financial statements and funder deliverables held by the associations (most for NBCTA and 4VI).
- **Permission or paid access (11 leads):**
  - BC Registries society filings, which hold incorporation records, name changes and director lists for each association;
  - Newspapers.com (Quesnel Cariboo Observer);
  - Times Colonist archive;
  - Destination BC Global Marketing Plan.
- **Organizational succession:** name changes are evidenced for each region (see `results/discovery_records/*/…_names.jsonl` and `sweep3`/`sweep4` organization-name records). Legal succession between 1980s associations and the current organizations is **not established**. Examples: KBVA → KCTA (1985, newspaper evidence); OSTA → TOTA (1997, organization's own history page); "North-By-Northwest" and "Peace River Alaska Highway" associations (Hansard 1989–1997), whose relation to NBCTA is not evidenced. Society registry filings would be the primary evidence.
